Project Title

Online Gemstone Store

This project aims to create a comprehensive online platform where users can explore, view detailed information, and purchase authentic gemstones with confidence.

Problem Statement

Identifying the current market challenges

Currently, customers face significant difficulties when searching for authentic gemstones online. The market lacks reliable platforms that provide comprehensive information, high-quality visual representation, and trustworthy authentication. Customers often struggle with:

  • Lack of detailed gemstone information and specifications
  • Poor quality images that don't accurately represent the gemstones
  • Difficulty verifying authenticity and quality certifications
  • Limited filtering and comparison options
  • Unclear pricing and purchase processes

Project Objectives

Online Platform

Develop a responsive and user-friendly online store for gemstones with secure payment integration.

Detailed Information

Provide comprehensive details including origins, properties, prices, and high-quality images for each gemstone.

Advanced Search

Implement intuitive searching and filtering by type, price, color, and rarity for easy navigation.
